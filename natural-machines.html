Natural Machines

Intern, Barcelona, Spain. June 2026 to July 2026.

Overview

  • Built and configured a ROS 2 Jazzy workspace on an NVIDIA Jetson for robotic arm development and integration.
  • Configured and tested CAN bus communication between the Jetson and Damiao motors using SocketCAN and UART, including flashing motor CAN IDs.
  • Supported hardware integration and troubleshooting, including BOM verification, mechanical assembly preparation, and modification and 3D printing of outer shell components.
  • Gained hands-on experience with ROS 2, NVIDIA Jetson, CAN bus, Linux, Git/GitHub, embedded systems, and robotic motor control.
