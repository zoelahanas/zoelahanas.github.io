About me

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at UC Berkeley and a Division I athlete, running both cross country and mid-distance track and field for Cal. Balancing a demanding engineering curriculum with 20+ hours of training and competition each week has taught me to manage my time carefully, show up when I'm tired, and keep a strong work ethic.

I love learning. Whether it's a new problem set, a CAD tool, or a robotics stack I've never touched, I enjoy digging in until I understand how it works. I'm passionate about using engineering to improve people's lives and experiences, and I want to work on real-world problems with meaningful solutions.

Outside the classroom, I love triathlons, running, and swimming (I was part of an ad for Katie Ledecky and Panasonic). I also love cooking, gardening, reviewing restaurants, and going on adventures.
